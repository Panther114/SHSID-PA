About

The SHSID Peer Advisors program connects students who excel in their coursework with peers who want to improve. Our guides are written and reviewed by experienced students to ensure accuracy and clarity.

Whether you're just starting out or preparing for advanced coursework, you'll find resources tailored to your level. New guides are added regularly across a growing range of subjects.

✍️

Student-Written

Every guide is authored by a fellow SHSID student who has excelled in that subject.

📊

Multi-Level

Guides are tagged Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced so you can find the right fit.

🎓

Always Free

All materials are freely available to every SHSID student, no sign-up required to read.
